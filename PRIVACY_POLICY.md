# SCRKPR — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 14, 2026_

SCRKPR is built to respect your privacy completely.

## The short version

**SCRKPR does not collect any data.** Everything you create in the app — players, scores, and game history — is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

## What we store, and where

- **On your device only:** the players you add (names, colors, emoji), in-progress and saved games, and your favorites/regulars. This data lives in your device's local storage and never leaves it.
- **We have no servers receiving your data,** because the app does not transmit your data anywhere.

## What we don't do

- We do not collect personal information.
- We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not share or sell any data (there's none to share).

## Deleting your data

You can remove everything at any time from **Settings → Clear All Data** in the app, or by deleting the app from your device.

## Children's privacy

SCRKPR does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
